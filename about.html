About

Genesis Core Systems: Banking beyond boundaries.

Community banks have been stuck choosing between rigid, decades-old cores and walled-garden platforms built for Tier-1 footprints. We built Genesis to close that gap: a modern core on terms that work for the institutions that serve Main Street.

Mission

Innovation meets execution.

Our mission is direct: take the technology that runs the world's largest banks and make it accessible to the institutions that serve Main Street. Not a watered-down version: Tier-1-grade architecture and capability patterns, sized and priced for community banks and delivered as a managed operating model rather than a DIY transformation program.

Want to talk it through? Send us a note.

What we believe

High-caliber tech accessible to all.

Genesis exists in the gap between legacy cores and platforms sized for the largest banks, and we believe that gap is where the next generation of community banking will be built.

We've spent careers inside the technology stacks that power the world's largest banks. We saw what worked, what didn't, and, most importantly, what was hoarded behind enterprise pricing models that smaller institutions could never justify. Genesis brings that capability into reach for community institutions, on terms that respect both their economics and their independence.

Principles

Four principles that guide every decision.

Community banks deserve better

The institutions that fund Main Street shouldn't be running on technology built for a different era. Genesis runs on a proven Tier-1-grade core foundation, configured for community banks, and everything we add around it, from conversion to support, is shaped around them.

Modularity over monoliths

Institutions should be free to adopt what they need now and add what they need later, without re-platforming. Every Genesis module stands alone and integrates cleanly with what surrounds it.

Open beats closed

Open APIs, open data, open vendor strategy. Lock-in is a tax community banks shouldn't have to pay, and a habit the industry has held onto for too long.

Service is not a department

Every Genesis colleague is responsible for customer success. We don't believe in throwing things over the wall. Implementation, support, and product all sit at the same table.

Leadership

Built by people who understand core banking, conversions, and community-bank operating realities.

Genesis was founded by Ohad Savir with Brian Brunner and Brent Jenos, both former Fiserv leaders with deep core-banking modernization experience, and Danny Galezer, whose background includes Sofgen and complex core implementation work. The team combines core-platform, implementation, integration, and bank-facing operating experience to bring Tier-1-grade capability into a model community banks can actually adopt.
