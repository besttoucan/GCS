03

Customizable Modules

From lending to payments, onboarding, reporting, and compliance outputs, Genesis modules are designed to be modular, API-first, and operationally practical for community banks. Managed cloud is the preferred Genesis operating model. For institutions with specific self-processing, security, or infrastructure requirements, Genesis can evaluate dedicated, hybrid, or bank-hosted/on-prem deployment patterns, subject to technical validation, security review, and final support constraints.

  • Managed cloud as the preferred baseline operating model.
  • Dedicated/private, hybrid, or bank-hosted deployment paths where justified by the bank's operating model and technically supportable.
  • Logical separation of bank data, configuration, and operating controls.
  • Bank-specific configuration without losing the ability to maintain an upgradeable platform.
  • Approved local extensions through APIs, adapters, and integration patterns.
  • Genesis-led integration, support coordination, and operational governance.
