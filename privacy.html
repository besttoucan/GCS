Privacy Policy.

Genesis Core Systems respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Genesis Core Systems ("Genesis," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit genesiscoresystems.com and interact with our forms and communications (the "Website").

1. Scope: What This Policy Covers and Does Not Cover

  • Website visitors and business contacts. This Privacy Policy DOES apply to information collected through the Website and our business communications, where Genesis acts as the controller of that information.
  • Customers of financial institutions. If you are a customer of a bank that uses Genesis technology, this Privacy Policy DOES NOT apply to your account information. That information is controlled by your financial institution. Please refer to your institution's privacy notice and contact them directly to exercise data rights, because Genesis CANNOT access or act on that data on their behalf.
  • Third-party sites. This Privacy Policy DOES NOT cover information collected by third-party websites or platforms you reach through links on the Website. Those platforms are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you complete our contact form or otherwise communicate with us, you may provide your name, email address, organization, and the contents of your message. Providing this information is voluntary, but some of it is necessary for us to respond.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Website, we and our analytics provider may collect standard technical information, including your IP address (used to derive approximate, city-level location), device and browser type, pages viewed, and referring links. This helps us keep the Website secure and understand how it is used.

We DO NOT knowingly collect sensitive personal information, such as government identifiers or financial account numbers, through the Website.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The Website uses a small number of cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are required for the site to function and are always active. Analytics cookies, provided through Google Analytics, are set only after you select Accept on our cookie banner; they help us measure visits and understand usage in aggregate. If you select Decline, analytics cookies are NOT set, and the full Website remains available to you.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings and can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out tools. We DO NOT use cookies for advertising.

6. How We Share Your Information

We DO NOT SELL or rent your personal information. We share it only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. With vendors that operate the Website, deliver our forms, and provide analytics, under agreements that require them to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.
  • Legal and safety. Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Genesis or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to the protections described in this Policy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@genesiscoresystems.com. You may unsubscribe from our communications at any time, and you may decline analytics cookies at any time using the cookie banner choice or your browser settings. We WILL NOT discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including responding to your inquiry and meeting legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. After that, it is deleted or de-identified.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we CANNOT guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

The Website is intended for a business audience and is not directed to children under 16. We DO NOT knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above and post the current version on this page. Please review it periodically.
